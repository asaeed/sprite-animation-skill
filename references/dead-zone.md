## Current Game-Specific Visual Requirements

These requirements belong to Dead Zone, not the general sprite methodology:

- Idle: natural neutral stance and forward-oriented boots, without inward twists or crossed legs.
- Gathering: distinct anatomical arms retaining their shoulder/sleeve identity; weapon stowed or a genuinely hands-free working pose. Visible reach/work/return without detached elbows.
- Gun shooting: rifle raised and shouldered, barrel aligned with the target, trigger hand on grip and support hand on fore-end; readable recoil and recovery.
- Zombie attacks: anticipation, hands approximately at eye height at the peak, forward strike and recovery, with readable movement in S/N as well as profiles.
- Melee/tool actions: role-specific larger arcs with controlled weight/torso movement and no newly exposed missing surfaces.

Evaluate the emitted artwork and playback, not only the rig's reported joint values. Preserve only still-approved clips while testing replacements. A 3D-to-sprite pilot remains a production experiment until model quality, matching appearance and motion have been inspected at game size.
